Common questions

Sparkamp doesn't show my music

Add the folder your music is in: Settings → Media Library → Add Folder…. Sparkamp reads every file in it, remembers the folder, and notices new music added there later. Add as many folders as you like.

A USB drive or a disc says it can't be read

In the Mac App Store version, macOS lets an app read a drive only after you choose it yourself. Click Grant Access…, leave the drive selected, and choose Grant Access. Sparkamp remembers the choice for that drive.

Which files does it play?

MP3, FLAC, AAC and M4A, WAV, AIFF, Ogg Vorbis and Opus. The Mac version needs macOS 26 or later.

Where does Sparkamp keep its settings and library?
  • Mac App Store version: ~/Library/Containers/com.sparkamp.sparkampmac/Data/Library/Application Support/sparkamp
  • Downloaded Mac version: ~/Library/Application Support/sparkamp
  • Linux: ~/.config/sparkamp

Quitting Sparkamp and deleting that folder starts it fresh. Your music files are not in it and are not touched.

What does Sparkamp send over the internet?

Very little, and nothing to the developer. The privacy policy lists every connection it makes.
