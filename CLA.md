This document follows the structure of the [Apache Individual Contributor
License Agreement](https://www.apache.org/licenses/icla.pdf), which most
contributor agreements descend from. The open-source-only restriction in
section 8 follows the approach [Signal](https://signal.org/cla/) takes.

---

## Why this exists

Sparkamp is published under the AGPL-3.0. A single person currently holds the
copyright on all of it, which is what makes it possible to distribute the
project through channels whose terms a copyleft licence cannot be handed to —
the Mac App Store among them.

That ability is not automatic. It exists only while the rights stay
consolidated. The moment a contribution arrives under the bare AGPL, the
project can no longer make those arrangements for that code without going back
and asking. Retrofitting agreement after the fact is the expensive version of
this conversation, and projects have lost years to it.

So: this asks you to grant rights, and section 8 limits what can be done with
them. You keep your copyright. Nothing here stops you using your own work
however you like, anywhere else.

Say so on your first pull request:

> I have read the Sparkamp CLA and I agree to it.

countersigned document, open an issue and it can be arranged.
